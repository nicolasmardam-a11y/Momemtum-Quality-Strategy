# Resolved-market universe (Gamma, endDate 2024-01-01 .. 2026-10-15)

Status: data build (family = data, verdict = n/a_data_build). As-of 2026-10-17.

## 0. Pre-registered acceptance checks (written 2026-10-17 before any counts were computed)

This is a data build, not a strategy test, so "rejection criteria" are data-quality acceptance checks.
The universe is accepted for downstream research only if all of the following hold; any failure is
reported in section 7 together with what it means for downstream tests.

1. Uniqueness: `condition_id` is unique after de-duplication by Gamma market `id` (month windows overlap at the boundary).
2. Parseability: >= 99% of markets have parseable `outcomes`, `outcomePrices` and `clobTokenIds` with equal lengths.
3. Resolution: >= 97% of markets classify as a clean winner (one payout = 1, rest = 0) or a 50-50 split;
   the remainder is listed by type.
4. Parent events: >= 99% of markets have a parent event found in `events_resolved.parquet`.
5. External cross-check: monthly counts of closed markets (2024-01 .. 2025-12) agree with an
   independent source (CryptoHouse `polymarket.assets`, distinct `market_condition_id` by end month)
   within +/-5% in at least 20 of 24 months; disagreements are explained or listed.
6. Category mapping: on markets where Gamma's `feeType` names a fee category, the tag/slug rule
   (computed WITHOUT looking at feeType) agrees with feeType on >= 90% of markets.

_Section 0 was written before any counts were computed and is left unchanged. Everything below was added
after the build finished (2026-10-17, ~23:00 UTC)._

**Sources.** All data comes from the Gamma `/markets/keyset` endpoint (`closed=true&include_tag=true&end_date_min/max`, 100 rows per page,
`after_cursor`), pulled in calendar-month windows, plus `/events/keyset` (`closed=true&include_markets=false`, event end-date
windows 2023-07..2026-10) and `/events/keyset?id=..` to backfill parents that were missing. Each raw page is cached under

**Per-market columns** (all those requested, plus the derived ones): `condition_id, question_id, slug, question, event_id,
event_slug, event_title` (the first, and in all but 9 cases the only, event embedded in the market payload), `tags` (market
tag slugs), `category` (+ `category_fee`, `category_rule`, `category_source`), `sportsMarketType, startDate, endDate,
closedTime, gameStartTime, umaResolutionStatus, umaResolutionStatuses, automaticallyResolved, outcomes, outcome_prices`
(resolution payouts), `clob_token_ids` (+ `token_id_0/1`), `negRisk, negRiskMarketID, negRiskOther, volumeNum, liquidityNum,
feesEnabled, feeType, feeSchedule` (+ parsed `fee_rate, fee_exponent, fee_rebate_rate, fee_taker_only`), `secondsDelay,
orderPriceMinTickSize, rewardsMinSize, rewardsMaxSpread, restricted, version`, plus `enableOrderBook, lastTradePrice,
volumeClob, umaBond, resolvedBy, event_*` fields and the derived fields below.

**Derived fields.**
- `resolution`: `winner` means exactly one payout is 1 and the rest are 0 (`winner_idx`, `winner_outcome`); `split_50_50` means the payouts
  are [0.5, 0.5]; otherwise `all_zero`, `unresolved_nonterminal` or `missing_prices`.
- Disputes: `has_disputed` is true when 'disputed' appears in `umaResolutionStatuses` or is the current status.
  `n_proposal_rounds` counts runs of consecutive 'proposed' records, and `multi_proposal` means more than one round.
  `dispute_flag` = `has_disputed OR multi_proposal`. `dup_proposal_record` marks the 2026-08+ recording artefact (section 5).
- `traded` is a proxy: `volumeNum > 0 OR lastTradePrice present` (section 9, item 1).
- `structure`: `neg_risk` when `negRisk` is true; `binary_grouped` for a non-neg-risk market whose event has more than one universe market;
  otherwise `binary_standalone`. All 4,152,445 markets have exactly 2 outcomes; neg-risk "multi-outcome" events are sets of binary markets.
- Leakage clusters: `cluster_id` is the sports game stem for sports. That stem is the event slug up to its ISO date: Gamma splits one game
  across several events, e.g. `bra-cor1-cre-2026-07-23`, `...-halftime-result`, `...-more-markets` and `...-player-props`. Outside sports
  the cluster is `event_id`, else `negRiskMarketID`, else `condition_id`. `cluster_week` is the ISO week of `endDate`. Downstream tests
  should block-bootstrap on `cluster_id` and on `cluster_week`.
- `split`, by `endDate`: train < 2025-07-01 <= validation < 2026-01-01 <= test (TEST ends at the 2026-10-15 universe end).
  `end_quarter`, `settle_lag_h` = closedTime - endDate, `settle_lag_game_h` = closedTime - gameStartTime, `duration_h`.

**Category mapping (deterministic).**
- `category_fee` maps the per-market `feeType` prefix to a category: sports, crypto, politics, finance, economics, culture, weather,
  tech, mentions or geopolitics, with `general` mapping to other. `zero_fees` falls through to the rule.
- `category_rule` never looks at feeType. It is a first-match tag/slug rule (see `categories.py`): mentions > sports
  (`sportsMarketType` present, a sports tag, or a league slug prefix) > crypto (a tag, or an up/down slug) > weather > culture-first tags
  (tweet counts, creators) > other-first tags (home prices) > election tags -> politics > economics (incl. earnings) > finance > tech >
  geopolitics > politics > culture > other.
- `category` = `category_fee` when available, else `category_rule`. feeType supplies the label for 0% of pre-2026 markets and for
  30% / 98% / 98% / 96% of markets ending in 2026Q1 / Q2 / Q3 / Q4. So **every train and validation label comes from the tag rule**.
- The tag sets were tuned on 2026-01..06 markets (feeType as the reference) and checked on 2026-07..10 (section 8, check 6). The rule's
  accuracy on 2024-25 tag vocabularies cannot be measured against feeType (**PLAUSIBLE**, not verified).

**Recording artefact (VERIFIED).** From August 2026 the status history usually reads `['proposed', 'proposed']`. The share is 0.3% for
markets ending 2026-07, 12% for 2026-08, 87% for 2026-09 and 92% for 2026-10. These markets show no dispute and are auto-resolved.
- The raw rule "more than one 'proposed'" would flag 45% of 2026Q3 and 92% of 2026Q4 markets as multi-proposal.
- `multi_proposal` therefore counts proposal *rounds*: runs of consecutive 'proposed' records. A real second round shows an intervening
  'disputed'.
- With that definition, `multi_proposal` implies `has_disputed` in every case. 28 markets are disputed with only one round.

## 9. Data quality issues

1. **`volumeNum` is null for 1,780,600 markets (43%), and null does not mean untraded** (VERIFIED by `/v2/trades` samples on 2026-10-17):
   - null volume: trades found in 20/25 crypto, 4/25 sports and 22/25 other markets;
   - null volume with a `lastTradePrice`: trades in 25/25;
   - both null: trades in 9/30 crypto, 1/30 sports and 0/30 other;
   - `volumeNum == 0`: trades in 0/30.
   `traded` (`volumeNum > 0 OR lastTradePrice present`) is therefore a proxy. It misses roughly 27k crypto markets (30% of 89,560) and
   roughly 50k sports markets (3% of 1.49M, wide CI). **Studies that need trade tapes should query `/v2/trades` rather than filter on
   Gamma volume.**
2. 1,584,464 markets (1,491,654 of them sports: props, totals, exact-score and the like, concentrated in 2026Q3-Q4) have neither volume
   nor a last trade price. They are
   listed but almost entirely untraded, which is why the all-markets counts overstate the investable universe.
3. `enableOrderBook=false` for 45,719 markets (10.6% of 2025Q4, 7.0% of 2026Q1). These were never on the CLOB, e.g. December-2025
   5-minute crypto Up/Down. Conversely, Gamma's closed-market keyset omits CLOB markets without an order book.
4. Sports `endDate` is a deadline rather than the game time (section 6). There are also 35,421 markets with endDate < startDate,
   2,174 with null startDate, 209 with null closedTime and 32 with closedTime < startDate.
5. The UMA status-history recording changed in 2026-08 (section 5). There are also 874 markets whose current `umaResolutionStatus` is
   'proposed', 50 with None and 2 with 'disputed', although their outcomePrices are terminal.
6. Coverage gaps (section 8):
   - markets with a null endDate are invisible to end-date windows (about 0.35% of on-chain-traded conditions in the sample);
   - about 0.5% of conditions are not served by Gamma;
   - 777 markets with endDate >= 2026-10-16 were dropped by the window filter.
7. Pull incidents, all handled and logged in `data/raw/gamma_universe/logs/`:
   - the 2026-08 monthly keyset chain hit a cursor that Cloudflare answers with HTTP 403 every time (reproduced with and without
     `include_tag`), so 2026-08 was pulled as 5 weekly windows;
   - `/events/keyset` returned HTTP 500 for the full 2026-09 month, so it was pulled weekly;
   - two concurrent runs in the interrupted session duplicated 2026-09w0. That chunk was moved to `redundant/` and used only for the
     containment check.
8. Several fields carry little information:
   - `version` = 'v1' for every market, so it does not mark the Protocol V2 era; use dates instead;
   - `restricted` = true for all but 15 markets;
   - `automaticallyResolved` = true for 99.95%;
   - `liquidityNum` is null for 54%.
9. Tick sizes: 0.001 for 83.5% of markets, 0.01 for 16.5%, 0.0025 for 159. `secondsDelay > 0` (an order delay) applies to 2.52M
   markets, mostly sports.
10. 454 markets have no tags. Pre-2026 categories rely on the tag rule alone (section 1).

## 10. What this means for downstream tests

- Filter on `traded` and `enableOrderBook`. Treat Gamma `lastTradePrice`, `bestBid`/`bestAsk` and `outcome_prices` as non-executable:
  they are final-state snapshots and resolution payouts, not entry prices.
- Cluster and bootstrap on `cluster_id` (sports game stem, event, negRisk group) and on `cluster_week`. TEST has 860k traded clusters
  but only 42 weeks; train has 79 weeks and validation 27. Week-level uncertainty, not market count, will dominate the confidence intervals.
- Train (45.6k markets, 40k traded) and validation (198k) are small next to TEST (3.9M), and their mix is different: more politics
  and neg-risk events, fewer short-horizon crypto and sports markets. Parameters tuned on train may not transfer, so report TEST by category.
- Fees in TEST were not constant (section 3). Apply the current schedule prospectively, and also report gross and as-traded results.
- Capital lock-up: use `settle_lag_game_h` for sports and `settle_lag_h` elsewhere. Disputed markets can lock capital for a week or more.
